04 ABOUT

Creator → Editor → Producer → Manager → Creative Partner

I started
with a camera.

A high-school advertising project introduced me to filmmaking, and I never really stopped creating.

I moved from screen production and photography into video editing, then into production, creative management and content strategy. Along the way, I started building systems around the creative process — SOPs, workflows and structures that made the work faster and more consistent.

That eventually led me into creative management, where I wasn't just making the work anymore. I was helping other creatives make better work too.

As AI changed the creative landscape, I leaned into it. I began building AI-assisted workflows and exploring new ways to make production faster and more scalable.

Today, I work across production, editing, strategy and AI-powered workflows — helping brands and content teams turn ideas into finished content.
