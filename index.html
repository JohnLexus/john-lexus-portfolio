01 / Discovery

Reel Content Finder

This is how I find reels instead of relying on clipping tools. It's personal to me, built to find the story inside the raw footage, not just cut a clip out of it.

  1. Raw video → txt file
  2. Analyze
  3. Transcript & cut points
  4. Virality rating
  5. High-SEO title & caption
02 / Gmail Organizer

Guest Research System

Built for podcast production: pulls the guest off the calendar, researches them, and gets the host a question set before they even ask for one.

  1. Google Calendar
  2. Check guest name & email
  3. Research
  4. Document & premade questions for host
  5. Sent to host a day before recording
03 / Release Pipeline

Podcast Release

Takes an episode from raw video to fully packaged, tested and ready for the edit to actually begin.

  1. Raw video
  2. Pre-cut
  3. Trailer script
  4. SEO titles & description
  5. Show notes
  6. Carousel (optional, pre-release)
  7. Thumbnail & title check
  8. A/B test titles & thumbnails
  9. Ready for editing
